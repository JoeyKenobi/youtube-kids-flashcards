# Chrome Web Store Listing Content

## Extension Name (max 45 chars)
YouTube Kids Flashcards

## Short Description (max 132 chars)
Pauses YouTube Kids videos with educational flashcard questions. Kids must answer 5 correctly to resume. 1,200 4th-grade questions.

## Detailed Description
Turn screen time into learning time! YouTube Kids Flashcards automatically pauses videos and presents fun, educational multiple-choice questions that kids must answer correctly before they can continue watching.

HOW IT WORKS:
• A flashcard quiz appears 15 seconds into each new video
• The child must answer 5 questions correctly to resume the video
• Questions appear again every 5 minutes during longer videos
• If the child fast-forwards more than 30 seconds, flashcards trigger again

SUBJECTS & CONTENT:
• 300 Mathematics questions (arithmetic, geometry, fractions, measurement)
• 300 Language Arts questions (grammar, vocabulary, reading comprehension)
• 300 Science questions (life science, earth science, physical science)
• 300 Social Studies questions (history, geography, civics, economics)
• All questions are designed for 4th-grade level

FEATURES:
• Beautiful overlay with progress bar showing how many correct answers remain
• Immediate feedback on each answer (correct/incorrect)
• No ads, no tracking, no data collection
• All data stored locally on your device
• Configurable interval timing
• Works seamlessly with YouTube Kids navigation

PERFECT FOR:
• Parents who want screen time to be educational
• Reinforcing school subjects during breaks
• Making video watching a rewarding activity

PRIVACY:
This extension collects NO personal data. All settings and answer history are stored locally on your device and never transmitted anywhere.

## Permission Justifications

### activeTab
Required to detect when a YouTube Kids video is currently playing so the extension can pause it and display educational flashcard questions.

### scripting
Required to inject the flashcard quiz overlay into YouTube Kids video pages so children can interact with the questions.

### storage
Required to save user preferences (enabled/disabled, interval timing) and local answer history for the stats display. All data remains on the user's device.

### Host permissions (youtubekids.com, youtube.com, youtube-nocookie.com)
Required to run the content script on YouTube Kids pages where videos are played. The extension must detect video playback and inject the flashcard overlay on these specific sites only.
